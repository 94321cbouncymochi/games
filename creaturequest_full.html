How to Play

🎮 Controls

  • WASD or Arrow keys — walk around. Hold Shift to run.
  • T Team · P Creaturedex · B Storage · I Bag · M Map · F Fly · Q Quests · K Cloud save · H Help · Esc close a menu
  • In battle: click a button or press 1–9. Click the message box or press Space to speed up text.
  • In battle, the 📊 Type Matchups box shows which elements are super effective against the enemy, how well each of your moves will do, and whether the enemy's type is dangerous to you. Press Y to show/hide it and C for the full type chart.
  • When you Switch, point at a creature to see its stats, HP, speed compared to the foe, and how well its moves work against the foe — plus a quick tip like 👍 Great pick! or ⚠️ Risky pick.

🌍 The world

  • The world goes on forever in every direction — meadows, forests, deserts, tundra, swamps, volcanoes, coasts and highlands.
  • The info box shows how much of your section you've explored (🧭 %). The map shows unexplored parts darker.
  • The world is split into sections of 512 × 512 tiles. Press M for a map of the section you're in — it shows biomes, shrines, trainers and you. Cross an edge and the map switches to the new section.
  • Wild creatures hide in tall grass, forest brush, sand dunes, snowy brush, swamp reeds, volcanic ash, rocky rubble and shallow water.
  • Each biome has its own creature types. A volcano has Flame and Stone creatures, the tundra has Frost and Wind, and so on.
  • Wild creatures are about as strong as your strongest creature. The HUD shows the level range.

🌍 World Level, 📋 Quests & no duplicates

  • World Level = the number of gyms you've beaten. Your creatures can only level up to World Level × 100 (at least 100), so beat gyms to raise the cap!
  • Wild creatures and trainers are about as strong as your strongest creature (between 10 levels lower and 10 levels higher). Houses and cave guardians are a bit stronger.
  • You can't own two of the same species. Creatures you already have can't be caught (the battle box says ✔ owned), eggs hatch into species you don't have, and a creature can't evolve into a species you already own.
  • Press Q for the Quest Board — 1,000,000 quests! Accept up to 5 at a time. They finish by themselves and pay coins (more coins at higher World Levels).

⚔️ Battling & catching

  • Pick Fight to use a move. Moves that are strong against the enemy's type do double damage.
  • To catch a creature, weaken it first, then choose Catch and throw an orb. Lower HP, status effects (burn, poison, freeze, paralysis) and better orbs make catching easier.
  • Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary. Rarer creatures are stronger but harder to catch. 1 in 300 is a ✨ Shiny with different colours!
  • Your 📦 Storage has tabs: Creatures, Boxes, Badges, Keepsakes and Eggs.
  • Winning gives XP and coins. Creatures level up and evolve 3 times — every family has 4 forms: Form 1 → Form 2 → Form 3 → MAX.
  • Each evolution gives a new name and look, keeps its level, boosts every stat, fully heals it, makes all its attacks stronger (★), and gives it a special trait (like 🛡️ Thick Hide or 💚 Regenerate). Some creatures even gain a second type!
  • Every creature has 5 attacks but can only equip 3 at a time. It starts with 3 unlocked; the 4th unlocks at its 1st evolution and the 5th at its 2nd. Change equipped attacks in Team → Info.
  • Trainers stand around the world. Walk into one to battle them for big coin rewards. You can't run from a trainer, but if a battle is going badly you can press 💸 Pay Up to hand over some coins and end it (no XP or prize, and you can challenge them again later).
  • ⚔️⚔️ Double battles! Trainers with a red 2v2 sign are tag teams — two trainers battle you together. Sometimes two wild creatures jump out at once, and some gym and house trainers battle in doubles too. You send out two creatures and pick a move for each one (and which foe to hit — hover a foe to see it light up). Whoever is fastest goes first. You need at least 2 healthy creatures for a double battle.

📦 Storage Box

  • Catch the same creature more than once? The Box shows ×2, ×3… on copies. Press 💰 Sell all duplicates to turn extra copies into coins. Your team, shinies and your best copy of each creature are always kept.

💗 Healing stations & 🛒 Shops

  • Healing stations (pink cross) fully heal your team and become your respawn point. If your whole team faints you go back there and lose half your coins.
  • Shops (blue-and-white stalls) sell 1,000 different items in 10 tabs: orbs, medicine, vitamins, battle items, lures, evolution stones, eggs, boxes, explorer gear and style (outfits, trails, dyes, titles). Better items unlock as you earn badges.
  • The HUD points to the nearest healer and shop. Compasses from the Explorer tab point to the nearest uncleared gym, cave, house or unbeaten trainer in the section you're in. Own all 6 compasses and arrows appear around you pointing at the closest things to clear. 🥾 Lava Boots let you walk on lava, 🩴 Water Boots let you walk across deep water, and a 🦘 Pogo Stick bounces you over trees, cactuses and mountains (Explorer tab).

🏟️ Gyms, badges & flying

  • Every 512 × 512 section has one gym near its middle (gold star on the map). Inside, trainers block the path — beat them and they run away. Then battle the Gym Master and their 6 creatures (as strong as yours, or a bit weaker).
  • Each gym gives a badge. 4 × 4 sections make a category of 16 badges. Every new badge gives you a box (better boxes for more badges), and all 16 give you a keepsake.
  • Press F to fly to the middle of any section whose gym you've beaten — just type the section numbers.

🏠 Houses & 🕳️ Caves

  • About 100 houses per section. Each one is different inside, with tough elite trainers guarding the doors and a treasure chest at the end.
  • Every mountain area has a cave. Caves are dark, full of wild creatures, have treasure chests, and a rare guardian creature hides at the very end.

🏘️ Towns, Cities & the Battle Tower

  • Every 512 × 512 section has 1 city and 3 towns (little gates with two houses and a red flag — they're on the M map too). Walk onto the gate to go inside.
  • Towns have streets, a fountain plaza, parks, a SHOP and a HEAL building (walk onto their doors). Talk to the people — some give tips, and some give you presents!
  • The Mayor (top hat, in front of the Town Hall) has 3 jobs for you: defeat certain creatures, beat trainers, catch creatures, deliver items, or show a creature. Finish all 3 to become the Hero of that town and get a big box.
  • 🗼 Battle Tower (in every city): Floor 1 starts at your level and each floor is 2 levels higher — forever! Win coins on every floor. Every 10th floor has a Tower Boss and a BIG treasure chest. No healing between floors (except healing pads every 5 floors). If you lose or leave, the climb ends, but you don't lose coins. Try to beat your best floor!

📜 The Story

  • Every 512 × 512 section has its own story chapter — the story never ends! Look for a person with a gold ! near a healing station or shop (follow the gold 📜 arrow). Talk to them, then go investigate where they send you.
  • Chapters are mixed from lots of pieces: you might find villain grunts, a tag team of grunts (double battle!), an Admin, an angry giant creature, your rival, or just a big mix-up. Then go back for your reward (and maybe a surprise).
  • Your rival remembers every battle: who won, and which of your creatures beat them. Next time they bring a creature to counter it!
  • The villain team has an evil plan with 9 steps. Beating their grunts and Admins pushes the story forward until you face their Boss. Then a new Act begins with a new leader and a new plan.
  • People you help become your friends. They send you gifts and sometimes show up again in another section.
  • The Dreamer Mystery: each section hides 3 clues that sparkle ✨ when you're close. Every 10 clues unlocks a Lore chapter about the secret history of the endless world (plus a reward).
  • Press J for your Story Journal: the current chapter, the villains' plan, your rival record, your friends and every Lore chapter you've unlocked.

🔥 Challenge Level

  • When any of your creatures reaches Lv 100, the Challenge Level unlocks. Press L (or 🔥 Challenge).
  • Set enemies anywhere from your best level − 100 (easier) up to your best level + 1000 (super hard!). It changes wild creatures, trainers, gyms, houses, caves and story battles.
  • The harder you set it, the more you get: up to ×6 coins and XP, plus a chance at a bonus 🎁 box after every win (+50 and up). Setting it easier gives less.

💾 Saving

  • The game saves automatically in this browser.
  • ☁️ Cloud save: connect a Google Doc (the setup screen shows you how). Then press ☁️ Save or K to put a save code in your doc. No Wi-Fi? The save is kept on this computer and uploaded automatically when Wi-Fi comes back. Load it on any computer with ⬇ Load from Doc.
